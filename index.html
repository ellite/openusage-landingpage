Features

Stop switching tabs to check your limits.

A focused, self-hosted dashboard built for developers and teams juggling multiple AI accounts.

Unified Usage View

See quotas, usage limits, account balances, and spending across all your AI providers in one clean dashboard — no more jumping between tabs.

Auto-Refresh

Data refreshes automatically every five minutes in the background. Manual refresh is always available, with cached data shown during provider outages.

Multi-Account Support

Add multiple accounts per provider. Ideal for teams or developers who manage several API keys, plans, or organizations at once.

OIDC / SSO

Authenticate with any OpenID Connect provider — Authelia, Authentik, Keycloak, and more. Drop it into your existing auth stack without friction.

Two-Factor Auth

Protect your dashboard with TOTP-based 2FA. Local accounts also support email-based password recovery for convenience.

Custom API Support

Track any provider not yet built-in by defining your own JSON field extraction rules. Future-proof your dashboard without waiting for an update.

PWA — Install Anywhere

Works as a Progressive Web App on any device. Install it on your phone for a native app experience, always at hand when you need to check your limits.

Push Notifications

Set a usage threshold per provider and get notified the moment you cross it. Stay ahead of quota limits before they interrupt your work.

Mobile

Your AI limits, always in your pocket.

Install OpenUsage as a PWA on your phone and get a fully native-feeling app. Check your remaining quotas and balances on the go — light or dark, whichever you prefer.

Self-Hosted

Your data. Your server.
Your rules.
